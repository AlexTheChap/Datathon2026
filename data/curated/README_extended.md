# AlphaForge, extended dataset and risk tools

This material builds on the starter dataset in `data/raw/` **without modifying it**. It contains:

- the prepared data for risk calculation (in CAD, aligned calendar, point-in-time);
- the risk tools (deterministic functions that return **evidence**);
- the governance rules and the personas;
- tests;
- scripts to fetch what is missing (10-K filings, ETF holdings).

> Simulation only. Nothing here is financial advice. Past returns do not predict future returns.

## Processing decisions

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| **Calendar** | Union of TSX and US trading days. Crypto is sampled on this calendar: the weekend return is included on Monday, which slightly understates its daily volatility. |
| **Market-specific holidays** | Last price carried forward, so the return is zero that day. Flag in `filled_flags.csv`. |
| **Currency** | Everything is converted to **CAD** (USD/CAD from the Bank of Canada, last rate carried forward on Canadian holidays). Local-currency returns are also provided. |
| **Returns** | Simple returns on `adj_close`: **total return**, dividends reinvested. |
| **CNQ, 2022-08-22** | `adj_close` kept. The gap with `close` (~2%) is consistent with a special distribution treated as a reinvested dividend. The 1.021 factor is **not** reapplied. To be confirmed with CNQ investor relations. |
| **Point-in-time macro** | `available_from` = **estimated** availability date: U.S. CPI month-end + 15 d, U.S. unemployment + 7 d, Canadian CPI + 21 d; daily series: next business day. **To be replaced** by the real dates (ALFRED, StatCan calendar). FRED values are revised. |
| **Survivorship bias** | The universe was chosen in 2026. Never present past returns as expected returns. |

Details and checks: `data/curated/quality_report.json`.

## Formulas (`risk_tools.py`)

- **Annualized volatility** = standard deviation of daily returns × √252; **downside volatility** = same calculation on negative returns only.
- **Maximum drawdown** = worst peak-to-trough decline, with the peak, trough and recovery dates (recovery time).
- **Historical VaR / CVaR 95%**, daily (and monthly in `asset_risk_metrics.csv`).
- **Beta** vs SPY and XIU.
- **Risk contribution** = wᵢ(Σw)ᵢ / wᵀΣw; **diversification ratio** = weighted average volatility / portfolio volatility.
- **Concentration**: Herfindahl index Σw² and effective number of positions 1/HHI. ETFs are decomposed if `holdings` is provided.
- **Stress / replay**: fixed allocation, rebalanced daily, replayed over the window (no fees or taxes).
- **max_position**: maximum weight of an asset (other positions scaled down pro rata) that keeps the scenario's maximum drawdown under the profile's limit.

## Evidence format (contract with the agents team)

Each tool returns:

```json
{"evidence_id": "ev-…", "tool": "stress", "inputs": {…}, "output": {…},
 "say": "In 2022 (…), this portfolio would have returned -12.9% (worst point -21.1%, in CAD)…",
 "currency": "CAD", "as_of": "…", "period": "2022-01-03/2022-12-30",
 "method": "…", "assumptions": ["…"], "data_sources": ["…"]}
```

- `say` is a short sentence, ready for the agent to speak, as text or voice.
- `as_of` cuts off all later data.
- `evidence_id` is stable: same inputs, same identifier.

## Results you can already check

**Data validation: empirical vs official duration** (`rate_sensitivity.csv`). The sensitivity of bond ETFs to rate changes, measured on our 2021 to 2026 data, matches the duration published by the issuers:

| ETF | Empirical duration | Official duration (Nov. 2026) | R² |
|---|---|---|---|
| XBB.TO | 6.6 | 6.74 | 0.93 |
| AGG | 5.9 | 5.70 | 0.91 |
| TLT | 14.2 | 14.75 | 0.83 |
| ZST.TO | 0.2 | 0.38 | 0.17 |

ZST's low R² is expected: its duration is almost zero, so noise dominates.

**Demo scenario, Robert** (simulated portfolio in `personas.json`, simulated capital of $400,000):

- Current portfolio in 2022: -4.6% (worst point -10.1%, i.e. -$40,264). Governance: **WARN** once the ETFs are decomposed: RY.TO = 10% direct + 2.9% via XIU = 12.9% (cap 10%). Without `etf_holdings.csv`: PASS.
- With 20% NVDA: -12.9% (worst point -21.1%, i.e. **-$84,232**). Governance: **BLOCK** (maximum loss and short-horizon rule).
- `max_position`: with his -15% limit, NVDA can go up to about **9%**. But the short-horizon rule (assets with volatility > 40% ≤ 5%) caps it at **5%**.

## Limits to show in the demo

- A single stress scenario in the current data (2022).
- Current durations, not historical.
- Estimated macro release lags.
- Survivorship bias.
- CURRENT ETF holdings (Nov. 2026 for SPY/XIU, June and August 2026 for QQQ/ESGV), applied to all dates; current GICS sectors. Without `etf_holdings.csv`, an ETF counts as a single position.
